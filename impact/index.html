How we got here.

2008

The vision

A young lady decides her life's issues and obstacles are not hers alone, but shared by so many other teenage girls. Helping Us Help Her begins.

2009

Established in Grand Prairie

HUHH is established with a mission to provide developmental tools that equip teenage girls to become morally excellent, and a promise: developing the You in her, so everyone will desire to know the Her in you.

Every year

The Walk-a-Thon becomes a tradition

A timed 5K and a morning walk through Grand Prairie for the girls. 2026 marked the 15th Annual HUHH 5K Walk-a-Thon at EPIC Central.

2016

The story on film

The Otis and Rosie Brown Foundation features Dr. Gray and HUHH in a short documentary about the program and the girls it serves.

Mentorship

The Mentorship Program

An inaugural mentorship program for older participants: a professional in her field of interest, career shadowing and internships, and support that continues past high school into college and a career.

2024

The first Golf Classic

Golfers, business leaders and sponsors gather for a day of friendly competition and giving. By 2026 it is the 3rd Annual HUHH Charity Golf Tournament at Waterchase Golf Club, with 100% of proceeds going to HUHH programs.

2026

Seventeen years in

The Choices Beyond Reality curriculum. Dream It Be It career support day in April. The 15th Annual 5K in May. The 3rd Golf Classic in July. And a mentorship track that keeps the door open after graduation.

Three programs, one purpose.

Practical, everyday approaches that help a girl understand herself, and bridge her from where she is to where her dreams can lead.

Beyond Promises and Choices

A school- and community-based program of counseling, mentoring and soft-skills training that improves the social-emotional, behavioral and educational competencies of students ages 13 to 18. The IMPACT module empowers adolescents to understand their purpose in life.

  • The Best In Me
  • What Is Beauty?
  • Quote/Unquote
  • Girl Talk
  • Relating to Relationships
  • Conflict Resolution
  • Future Me
  • Ideal Woman

Choices Beyond Reality

Helps teenagers face the realities they live in and rise past them, with lessons that adapt to each student and a curriculum built to run inside school districts and colleges. Its aim: critical thinking, cultural competence, emotional resilience and community engagement.

  • Critical thinking
  • Cultural competence
  • Emotional resilience
  • Community engagement

The Mentorship Program

For older HUHH participants: one-on-one mentor relationships, career shadowing and internships, and another point of engagement that extends the HUHH platform beyond high school.

  • Finding your career path
  • Understanding your communication style
  • Leadership skills
  • College preparation
  • Managing college life
  • Managing virtual learning
  • Interview skills
  • Resume skills
  • Networking, online and in person
  • Online presence (LinkedIn and social)
  • Business and professional relationships
