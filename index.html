She already has a purpose. We help her find it.

Vision

Empowering girls to value their self worth and become valuable and successful women in life.

Mission

Through H.U.H.H., teen girls become capable of defining themselves, valuing their self worth and set on the right path to achieving their goals and DREAMS in life.

How

Educating, exposing and preparing young ladies, daily, to value their self worth in knowing who they are.

Seventeen years of showing up for her.

What began in 2009 through the vision of one young woman is now a curriculum, a mentorship track, a 5K in its fifteenth year and a golf classic in its third.

Established 2009

Founded through the vision of a young lady who felt her obstacles were not hers alone, but shared by so many other teenage girls.

Beyond Promises and Choices

A school- and community-based curriculum of counseling, mentoring and soft-skills training for girls 13 to 18, built around eight impact modules.

Fifteen years of the 5K Walk-a-Thon

The Mentorship Program

Older HUHH participants matched with professionals in their field of interest, with support that carries from college into their careers.

The HUHH Golf Classic

The 3rd Annual Charity Golf Tournament teed off at Waterchase Golf Club on July 17, 2026, with 100% of proceeds going to HUHH programs.

Eight modules. One girl at a time.

The Beyond Promises and Choices IMPACT curriculum, in the order a girl meets it. Hover or tap a module.Tap a module.

The Best In Me

A reflection of who she is. Naming how others see her, and deciding for herself what really matters.

What Is Beauty?

Manners, morals and makeup. The M3 of carrying yourself, with etiquette and values as a guide for everyday life.

Quote/Unquote

The need to know. Honest talk about social norms, and the decisions that sit behind them.

Girl Talk

A safe circle for the unspoken words of teens, and the lesson that thoughts and actions shape a future.

Relating to Relationships

Family, friends and connections. Seeing the difference between them, and knowing her own limits.

Conflict Resolution

Finding peace in the middle of the storm, through exercises, examples and one-on-one conversations.

Future Me

Academic readiness, leadership and wealth building. Goal setting, college and career prep, and financial literacy.

Ideal Woman

Dress, presence and seizing the opportunity. Professional standards for a life she designs herself.

Parents and school staff: the curriculum runs on campus and in the community, for girls 13 to 18. Ask at info@huhh.org.
